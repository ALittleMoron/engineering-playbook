# Task Estimation

- Assign every task an estimate between 8 and 16 hours.
- Round work estimated at 2–4 hours up to 8 hours.
- Decompose work estimated at more than 16 hours into smaller tasks, each with an independently
  verifiable outcome.
- Base estimates on the time required by a developer who knows the project and works without AI
  assistance.
- Include writing and running tests in every implementation estimate.
- Account for manual investigation, implementation, and result verification. Do not reduce the
  estimate to reflect AI code generation.
- Do not invent work solely to reach the minimum estimate.
